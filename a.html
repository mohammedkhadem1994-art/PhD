About Me

I am Mohammed Khadem, a Mechatronics Engineer and researcher with a PhD in Mechatronics Engineering from the University of August 20, 1955, Skikda, Algeria.

I previously conducted research at the Department of Mechanical, Energy and Management Engineering (DIMEG), University of Calabria, Italy. My research appointment ran from May 2024 to April 2026.

My work combines mechanical design, mathematical modeling, simulation, control systems, prototyping, and robotic applications. My interests include intelligent robotics, autonomous systems, UAV inspection, and practical engineering solutions.

Research Interests

My research spans robotics, mechatronics, intelligent control, and autonomous systems.

Cable-Driven Parallel Robots

Design, kinematic modeling, trajectory generation, workspace analysis, and control of cable-driven robotic systems.

Rehabilitation Robotics

UAVs and Drones

Artificial Intelligence

Autonomous Systems

Mission planning, energy-aware operation, intelligent inspection, and robotic autonomy.

Published Research

1. A Pyramidal Cable-Driven Robot for Rehabilitation

This research investigates the design and control of a pyramidal cable-driven parallel robot intended for writing and drawing exercises in rehabilitation. The work combines robotic structure design, modeling, and motion-control considerations.

UAV Research

2. Reconfigurable UAVs for Structural Inspection

Research on reconfigurable unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) for inspection and thermal monitoring of buildings and cultural heritage sites. The concept features protective panels that transform into landing legs, helping protect the drone during contact with nearby surfaces while supporting landing operations.
