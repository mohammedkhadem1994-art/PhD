About Me

I am Mohammed Khadem, a Mechatronics Engineer and researcher with a PhD in Mechatronics Engineering from the University of August 20, 1955, Skikda, Algeria.

I previously conducted research at the Department of Mechanical, Energy and Management Engineering (DIMEG), University of Calabria, Italy. My research appointment ran from May 2024 to April 2026.

My work combines mechanical design, mathematical modeling, simulation, control systems, prototyping, and robotic applications. My interests include intelligent robotics, autonomous systems, and practical engineering solutions.

Research Interests

My research interests span robotics, mechatronics, intelligent control, and autonomous systems.

Cable-Driven Parallel Robots

Proposed Research Concept

3. Autonomous Battery Replacement for UAVs

This proposed research explores autonomous battery exchange stations that could enable inspection drones to replace depleted batteries and resume missions with less human intervention. The concept targets longer-duration robotic inspection.

Status: Proposed research concept. Implementation and experimental validation should only be claimed once completed.
