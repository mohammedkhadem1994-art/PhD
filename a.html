About Me

I am Mohammed Khadem, a Mechatronics Engineer and researcher with a PhD in Mechatronics Engineering from the University of August 20, 1955, Skikda, Algeria.

I previously conducted research at the Department of Mechanical, Energy and Management Engineering (DIMEG), University of Calabria, Italy. My research appointment ran from May 2024 to April 2026.

My work combines mechanical design, mathematical modeling, simulation, control systems, prototyping, and robotic applications. My research interests include intelligent robotics, autonomous systems, UAV inspection, and practical engineering solutions.

I am interested in academic collaboration, interdisciplinary research, and the development of robotic technologies addressing real-world engineering challenges.

UAV Research

2. Reconfigurable UAVs for Structural Inspection

This research explores a reconfigurable protective mechanism for unmanned aerial vehicles used in structural inspection and thermal monitoring of buildings and cultural heritage sites.

The concept uses protective panels that can change configuration from a protective shield around the drone into landing legs. The mechanism is intended to support safer interaction with nearby surfaces and provide a landing configuration when required.

Related research directions include drone stabilization, visual and thermal inspection, structural-condition monitoring, and autonomous inspection planning.

Research Concept

3. Autonomous Battery Replacement and Mission Continuation

A proposed research direction for long-duration UAV inspection. The concept focuses on energy-aware mission planning, autonomous return to a battery replacement station, mission-state storage, and resumption of an interrupted inspection task.

This topic is intended as a future research direction; its description should not be interpreted as evidence that a complete autonomous battery-swapping system has already been built or experimentally validated.
