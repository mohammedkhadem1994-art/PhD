About the Laboratory

Connecting robotics, engineering and intelligence

A research initiative designed to support scientific exchange, interdisciplinary projects and international collaboration.

The International Laboratory for Robotics, Mechatronics and Artificial Intelligence (ILRMAI) is a developing research initiative focused on the design, modeling, simulation and intelligent control of advanced robotic and mechatronic systems.

Its research vision brings together robotics, mechanical engineering, electronics, control theory and artificial intelligence to address complex engineering challenges.

The initiative aims to foster scientific cooperation among researchers, engineers, doctoral students and academic institutions through research projects, technical resources and knowledge exchange.

Current status: Virtual Research Initiative. ILRMAI is not yet a formally registered or institutionally accredited entity. Partnerships, membership and any future physical facilities will be announced only once they are established.

Our Mission

To advance research and knowledge in robotics, mechatronics and artificial intelligence through rigorous engineering, open scientific exchange and collaborative innovation.

Our Vision

To develop a sustainable research community that connects scientific ideas with practical robotic systems and real-world engineering applications.

Our Values

Scientific integrity, reproducibility, interdisciplinary research, responsible innovation and international cooperation.

Research Areas

Researching intelligent engineering systems

The laboratory's proposed research areas span the design, analysis and control of robotic systems, from mechanical architectures to autonomous decision-making.

⚙️

Robotics and Mechatronics

Robotic mechanisms, mechatronic design, kinematic and dynamic modeling, prototyping and system integration.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, intelligent perception and AI-assisted engineering.

🚁

UAVs and Autonomous Systems

Autonomous aerial systems, UAV inspection, mission planning, sensing, stabilization and energy-aware operation.

🎛️

Control and Automation

Intelligent control, nonlinear systems, trajectory tracking, optimization, simulation and experimental validation.

🦾

Rehabilitation Robotics

Assistive robotic systems, cable-driven parallel robots, human-centered mechanisms and rehabilitation applications.

🔬

Modeling and Prototyping

CAD-based design, numerical modeling, simulation, rapid prototyping, testing and performance evaluation.

Join the Initiative

Collaborate with ILRMAI

ILRMAI is at an early stage and is not presenting an existing international team. Collaboration requests are welcome from the following groups.

Researchers

Academics interested in joint papers, shared methods or co-supervised work in robotics, control and AI.

Doctoral students

PhD candidates seeking scientific exchange, feedback on research ideas or collaboration on shared topics.

Engineers

Practitioners interested in prototyping, testing and applying robotic and mechatronic solutions.

How collaboration works (proposed process)

  1. Send an enquiryDescribe your background and the topic of interest through the form below.
  2. Initial reviewYour request is checked for scientific fit with the research themes.
  3. Define the scopeGoals, roles and expected outputs are agreed in writing.
  4. Formalise when possibleMemberships and institutional partnerships will follow once a formal entity exists.

Contacting ILRMAI or taking part in a discussion does not create formal membership, employment, accreditation or institutional affiliation. Any formal status would be agreed in writing. The process above may change as the initiative develops.
